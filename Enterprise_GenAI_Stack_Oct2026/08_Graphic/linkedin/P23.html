The firm's own records: evidence, configuration, datasets, credentialsdarker cells: an exit becomes a reconstruction
Lock-in to a product is usually tolerable; lock-in of the firm's own records is not. Classify each layer once, record it in the exit plan, and review it when ownership changes. Generic: no vendor names.
