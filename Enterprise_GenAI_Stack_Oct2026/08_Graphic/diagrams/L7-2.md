# L7 decision tree: choosing embedding and reranking models for a corpus
Caption: Data sensitivity and permitted processing location decide the embedding route first, then reranking, hybrid retrieval and multimodal needs are settled, and every choice is version-pinned and migrated behind an L9 regression gate.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  classDef note fill:#FBF4E4,stroke:#D4A13A,stroke-dasharray:4 3;
  START["START: a corpus to make retrievable<br/>(one decision per corpus / index)"] --> Q1{"1. Does the corpus contain<br/>client, personal or<br/>confidential data?"}
  Q1 -- "no: any approved hosted<br/>API is acceptable" --> Q3
  Q1 -- "yes" --> Q2{"2. Where may it<br/>be processed?"}
  subgraph S2[" "]
    Q2 -- "only inside our estate<br/>(or air-gapped)" --> V{"Need vendor<br/>support?"}
    V -- "yes, NVIDIA AI Enterprise<br/>already licensed" --> E1["NVIDIA NeMo Retriever NIMs"]
    V -- "yes, otherwise" --> E2["Cohere private / on-prem deployment<br/>(or Jina on-prem licence if Elastic is the store)"]
    V -- "no" --> E3["Sentence Transformers serving an Apache-2.0 model<br/>(Qwen3 only after provenance review;<br/>voyage-4-nano is another option)"]
    Q2 -- "UK processing<br/>required" --> UK["None of the hosted APIs here verified UK processing for embeddings → treat as 'own estate' (above) or a VPC/marketplace deployment in a UK region (Cohere on SageMaker/Foundry, Voyage on SageMaker); confirm the region"]
    UK -. "own estate" .-> V
    Q2 -- "EU processing<br/>acceptable" --> EU{"Which estate<br/>or store?"}
    EU -- "store is MongoDB" --> U1["Voyage via Atlas EEA Geography<br/>(accept preview status, obtain the SOC 2 report)"]
    EU -- "store is Elasticsearch" --> U2["Jina via Elastic Inference Service<br/>(pin the model)"]
    EU -- "Google-centred estate,<br/>multimodal" --> U3["Gemini Embedding 2<br/>on the 'eu' endpoint"]
    EU -- "OpenAI already approved,<br/>text-only" --> U4["text-embedding-3 on an<br/>EU project with ZDR"]
    EU -- "otherwise" --> U5["Cohere<br/>(SaaS with ZDR, or VPC)"]
  end
  S2 --> Q3{"3. Do we need a reranker?<br/>(almost always yes for<br/>precision-critical answers)"}
  Q3 -- "same vendor offers one and<br/>it passes in-domain eval" --> R1["Use it"]
  Q3 -- "embedding vendor has none in the same API<br/>(OpenAI; Google's is the Vertex ranking API)" --> R2["Cohere Rerank 4, Voyage rerank-3 (Preview),<br/>or a self-hosted cross-encoder"]
  Q3 -- "store hosts it<br/>(MongoDB $rerank, Pinecone, Elastic)" --> R3["Acceptable if the model version is pinned<br/>and logged, and entitlement filtering precedes it"]
  R1 --> Q4
  R2 --> Q4
  R3 --> Q4
  Q4{"4. Exact identifiers matter<br/>(fund codes, ISINs,<br/>share classes)?"} -- "yes" --> H["Hybrid first stage<br/>(BM25/sparse + dense, fused)<br/>before rerank"]
  Q4 -- "no" --> Q5
  H --> Q5{"5. Scanned pages,<br/>charts or images<br/>matter?"}
  Q5 -- "yes" --> M["Multimodal model (Gemini Embedding 2, Cohere Embed 5, Jina v5-omni, voyage-multimodal-3.5, NVIDIA VL), chosen by in-domain eval"]
  ALL["6. Always: pin versions in C5, keep raw text, tag vectors with model_version, and migrate by dual index behind an L9 regression gate."]:::note
  Q5 -- "no" --> ALL
  M --> ALL
```
