Search-tool summary of mem0.ai pages: Mem0 describes itself as SOC 2 (Type 1), HIPAA, and GDPR compliant. Its Trust Center is linked from the site navigation. One comparison page says Mem0 also offers SOC 2 Type I and HIPAA compliance on managed cloud. The company lists managed cloud, self-hosted Docker, private Kubernetes, and fully air-gapped deployment, and says all models use the same API. A health-focused post describes the Kubernetes/private-cloud option as one that deploys inside your VPC with the same API surface; no data leaves your infrastructure. Mem0 says all deployment models support BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) encryption. The pages don't state clearly whether SOC 2 and HIPAA coverage extends to self-hosted or air-gapped deployment. Mem0's own enterprise guide notes that a security team should validate these controls against its own policies before approval.
