Search-tool summary: Mem0 has four tiers: a free Hobby plan, Starter at $19/month, Pro at $249/month, and a custom-priced Enterprise plan. Hobby (free): 10,000 add requests and 1,000 retrieval requests per month, with one project; 'The Hobby plan is permanently free - no credit card required.' Starter ($19/month): 50,000 add requests and 5,000 retrieval requests per month, one project. Pro ($249/month): 500,000 add requests and 50,000 retrieval requests per month, unlimited projects, advanced analytics, private Slack support, graph memory (entity linking), and Dream (memory consolidation). Enterprise (custom): unlimited requests and projects, plus on-prem deployment, audit logs, SSO, custom integrations, and SLA support. The site also mentions usage-based pricing as an option.
