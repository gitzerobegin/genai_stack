Search-tool extract: 'The contractual arrangements on the use of ICT services shall include at least the following elements': a clear and complete description of the services; whether subcontracting is permitted and under which conditions; the locations (regions or countries) where services are provided and data processed, including storage, with notice of changes; provisions on availability, authenticity, integrity and confidentiality including personal data; access, recovery and return of data on insolvency, resolution, discontinuation or termination; service level descriptions; assistance in ICT incidents at no additional cost or at a pre-determined cost; full cooperation with competent and resolution authorities; termination rights and related minimum notice periods; participation in the financial entity's ICT security awareness and digital operational resilience training. Article 30(3), for critical or important functions, adds (fragments in extract): exit strategies with a transition period, and termination triggers such as a significant breach by the ICT third-party service provider of applicable laws, regulations or contractual terms; financial entities identify alternative solutions and develop transition plans. Where TLPT participation could harm service quality or confidentiality for other customers, the provider may contract directly with an external tester under the financial entity's direction; pooled testing may be used.
