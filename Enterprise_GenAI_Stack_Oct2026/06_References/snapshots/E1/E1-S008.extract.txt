Search-tool extract (several reproductions of the text agree): Article 12(1): products with digital elements classified as high-risk AI systems under Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 that fulfil the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I Part I, where the manufacturer's processes comply with Annex I Part II, and where the achieved level of cybersecurity protection is demonstrated in the EU declaration of conformity, are deemed to comply with the cybersecurity requirements of Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, without prejudice to the other requirements of that Article (accuracy and robustness). Article 12(2): the conformity assessment procedure of Article 43 of the AI Act applies. Article 12(3): by way of derogation, important (Class I/II) and critical products subject to CRA Article 32 conformity procedures that would use AI Act internal control (Annex VI) remain subject to the CRA conformity assessment procedures for the essential cybersecurity requirements.
