Search-tool extracts:
- Article 23(4): early warning 'without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of the significant incident'; incident notification within 72 hours; final report 'not later than one month after the submission of the incident notification'. Trust service providers notify within 24 hours for incidents affecting their trust services.
- Article 21: measures must be appropriate and proportionate and include, among others, policies on risk analysis and information-system security, incident handling, business continuity and crisis management, and 'supply chain security, including security-related aspects concerning the relationships between each entity and its direct suppliers or service providers'. Recitals encourage entities to incorporate cybersecurity risk-management measures into contractual arrangements with direct suppliers and service providers.
- Article 20: management bodies approve the cybersecurity risk-management measures, oversee their implementation and can be held liable for infringements; members must follow training.
- Article 34: for essential entities, maximum fines of at least EUR 10 000 000 or at least 2% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher; for important entities EUR 7 000 000 or at least 1.4%.
- Article 3: essential entities include Annex I entities exceeding the medium-sized enterprise ceilings, plus qualified trust service providers, TLD name registries and DNS service providers regardless of size; the size-cap rule brings medium-sized and larger entities into scope, small and micro entities generally excluded.
- Jurisdiction: DNS service providers, TLD registries, cloud computing service providers, data centre service providers, CDN providers, managed service providers, managed security service providers, online marketplaces, search engines and social networking platforms fall under the Member State of their main establishment in the Union (where cybersecurity risk-management decisions are predominantly taken; failing that, where cybersecurity operations are carried out; failing that, the establishment with the most employees in the Union).
- Transposition: by 17 October 2024.
