Search-tool extract (practitioner commentary): the 2026 Commission guidance turns the Article 3(2) definition into a practical test: is the processing at a distance, would its absence prevent a function of the product, and was the software designed by or under the manufacturer's responsibility. A web application reached only through a browser is not a product merely because it is accessed remotely; the guidance does not create a general SaaS exclusion. Standalone SaaS or cloud services built outside a manufacturer's responsibility are not products with digital elements, but they fall in scope if they meet the remote data processing definition for a product.
