Search-tool extract: Free and open-source software falls in scope only when it is made available in the course of a commercial activity; unmonetised open-source products are not treated as commercial activity, and individual developers contributing to projects not under their responsibility are excluded. The CRA creates the category of 'open-source software stewards' (legal persons that systematically support the development of free and open-source software intended for commercial activities). Stewards have the lighter Article 24 obligations (cybersecurity policy, cooperation with market surveillance authorities, reporting) and 'are not subject to administrative fines for infringements of the CRA' (Article 64(10)).
