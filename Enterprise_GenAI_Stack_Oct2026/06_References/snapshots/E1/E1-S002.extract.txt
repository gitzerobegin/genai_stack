Search-tool extract: 'The main obligations introduced by the Act will apply from 11 December 2027, with reporting obligations to apply as of 11 September 2026.' The Commission published practical guidance to help manufacturers, developers and businesses of all sizes meet their obligations under the Cyber Resilience Act on 27 July 2026. Manufacturers can affix the CE marking and attach the EU declaration of conformity when placing the product on the market; afterwards they must handle vulnerabilities for the support period and report actively exploited vulnerabilities.
