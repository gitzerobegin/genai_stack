Search-tool extract: the Data Act is applicable from 12 September 2025. Chapter VI covers providers of data processing (cloud and edge) services. Switching charges, including data egress charges, are removed entirely from 12 January 2027; until then providers may charge switching costs as a transitional measure. Providers will not be able to charge customers for operations necessary to facilitate switching or for data egress. IaaS providers must take measures so customers obtain materially comparable outcomes for shared features (e.g. tools for shifting workloads between virtualisation technologies); PaaS and SaaS providers must offer open interfaces and, at a minimum, export data in a machine-readable format. The Commission has published FAQs and a Data Act Legal Helpdesk.
