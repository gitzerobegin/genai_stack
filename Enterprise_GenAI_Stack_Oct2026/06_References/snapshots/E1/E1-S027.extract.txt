Search-tool extract: the Commission's Digital Omnibus proposal (19 November 2025) would exempt from the switching provisions 'custom-made services other than Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS)' under contracts concluded on or before 12 September 2025, and create a lighter regime for PaaS and SaaS provided by SMEs and small mid-caps under such contracts. It proposes repeal of Regulation (EU) 2018/1807 (free flow of non-personal data) as switching is addressed in the Data Act. Status: proposal.
