Search-tool extract: the enacted text defines a deployer as 'a person who deploys an artificial intelligence system for use in this state' and a developer as a person who develops an AI system offered, sold, leased or otherwise provided in Texas. The Department of Information Resources runs a regulatory sandbox; per the committee analysis a participant may test and deploy an AI system for not more than 36 months, and the AG or a state agency may still act against a participant that breaches the prohibitions. The enrolled bill summary also mentions disclosure requirements for certain government and health-care users and creation of the Texas Artificial Intelligence Council. Some numbers (cure periods) were reported from introduced or committee versions.
