Search-tool extract: the joint RTS on subcontracting (published in the Official Journal in 2025) sets conditions for subcontracting ICT services supporting critical or important functions: the provider identifies all subcontractors in the chain; the financial entity receives the same access and audit rights through the chain; the provider gives notice of material subcontracting changes early enough for the entity to object; the contract allows termination if the provider implements a material change despite the entity's objection.
