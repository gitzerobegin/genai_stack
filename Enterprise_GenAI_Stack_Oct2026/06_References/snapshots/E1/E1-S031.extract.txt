Search-tool extract (consolidated text as amended): in the case of SMEs, including start-ups, each fine referred to in Article 99 is up to the percentages or amount referred to in paragraphs 3, 4 and 5, 'whichever thereof is lower'. For small mid-cap enterprises (SMCs) the same lower-of rule applies only to paragraphs 4 and 5.
