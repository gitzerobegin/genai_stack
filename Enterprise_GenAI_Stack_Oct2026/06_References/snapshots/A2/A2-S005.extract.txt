Uses Matryoshka Representation Learning; default 3072 dimensions; recommended 3072, 1536, 768; flexible output dimension size supporting 128 - 3072; auto-normalizes truncated dimensions. gemini-embedding-2 produces a single aggregated embedding when multiple inputs are provided in one request. task_type field not supported.
