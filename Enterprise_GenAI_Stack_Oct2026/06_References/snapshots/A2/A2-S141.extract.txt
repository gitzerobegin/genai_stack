About one week old (c. 30 September 2026): rerank-3 and rerank-3-lite; vendor claim rerank-3 outperforms Cohere Rerank v4.0 Pro by 2.14% and Qwen3-Reranker-8B by 3.31% overall. $rerank is an index-less aggregation stage that rescoring candidates with rerank-3 or rerank-3-lite.
