December 2025 What's New: Amazon S3 Vectors is now generally available with 40 times the scale of preview. With general availability, you can store and query up to two billion vectors per index and elastically scale to 10,000 vector indexes per vector bucket. At GA the service launched in 14 AWS Regions, expanding from 5 Regions in preview. GA announcement cited retrieve up to 100 search results per query and about 1,000 vectors per second single-vector writes.
