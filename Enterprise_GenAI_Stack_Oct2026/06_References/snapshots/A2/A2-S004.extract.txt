General availability came on April 22, 2026, when Google announced it via the Gemini API and Vertex AI. The stable model ID is gemini-embedding-2. Earlier launch blog (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-embedding-2/): 'Gemini Embedding 2: Our first natively multimodal embedding model' - maps text, images, video, audio, and documents into one shared space; over 100 languages; launch blog cites up to 120 seconds of video input in MP4 and MOV formats.
