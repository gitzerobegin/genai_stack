Our Rerank models are priced based on the quantity of searches, and our Embedding models are priced based on the number of tokens embedded. Up-to-date prices on the dedicated pricing page. Standard Vault Pricing (https://docs.cohere.com/docs/model-vault/standard/pricing): Embed 5 Pro Small is $3.00/hour or $2,000/month, and Rerank 4 Pro Large is $10.00/hour or $6,500/month, per instance.
