The Voyage 4 series (launched January 2026) includes voyage-4-large, voyage-4, voyage-4-lite, and the open-weighted voyage-4-nano. voyage-4 approaches the retrieval quality of voyage-3-large while maintaining the efficiency of a mid-sized model. All four models share an embedding space; embeddings generated from different models can be used interchangeably (e.g., documents with voyage-4-large, queries with a smaller model). Supports multiple embedding quantization options, including binary.
