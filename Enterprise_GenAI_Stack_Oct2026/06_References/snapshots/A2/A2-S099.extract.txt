Monthly minimum commitment of $50/month for Standard and $500/month for Enterprise. Serverless billing covers reads, writes, storage, and egress. A query uses 1 RU for every 1 GB of namespace size, minimum 0.25 RUs per query. Storage billed per GB per month.
