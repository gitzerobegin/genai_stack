Press release dated October 9, 2025: Elastic (NYSE: ESTC), the Search AI Company, has completed the acquisition of Jina AI, a pioneer in open source multimodal and multilingual embeddings, reranker, and small language models. Complements Elastic's ELSER model; brings rerankers for visual and long-context multilingual documents; small models for HTML-to-Markdown conversion; expands Elastic's AI research team. SEC filings give closing date October 7, 2025; FY2026 10-K purchase consideration $43.4 million for Conic AI Technology Limited (Jina AI).
