Cohere announced Embed 5 on September 30, 2026. Model IDs embed-v5.0-pro and embed-v5.0-fast. Pro aimed at highest retrieval accuracy, especially offline indexing; Fast targets low latency and high query volume (interactive search, agent loops). Both tiers map into the same embedding space; Cohere recommends indexing with Pro and querying with Fast. Embed text, images, and mixed text-and-image inputs (such as PDF pages) into one vector; over 100 languages; 128k-token context window; Matryoshka outputs 256 to 2048 dimensions; float, int8, and binary formats. Available through Cohere's API and Model Vault, Microsoft Foundry, and Amazon SageMaker; Model Vault covers single-tenant deployments.
