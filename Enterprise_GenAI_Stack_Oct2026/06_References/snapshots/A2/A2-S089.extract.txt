========================================================================

Bedrock Knowledge Bases supports S3 vector buckets as a vector store; requires the vector bucket ARN and vector index ARN. Bedrock caps custom metadata at 1 KB per vector (filterable plus non-filterable) and 35 metadata keys per vector; if exceeded the ingestion job throws an exception. Bedrock computes and stores the vectors and adds the source text chunk as large metadata. CloudFormation: AWS::Bedrock::KnowledgeBase S3VectorsConfiguration.
