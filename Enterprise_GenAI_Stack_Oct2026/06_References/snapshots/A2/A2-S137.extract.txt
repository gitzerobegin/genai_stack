General availability of MongoDB Search (full-text) and Vector Search for MongoDB Enterprise Advanced and MongoDB Community Edition (post ~3 months old, i.e. c. July 2026). Requires MongoDB 8.2 or later; runs on separate binary mongot alongside mongod. $search, $searchMeta, $vectorSearch, $rankFusion, $scoreFusion with functional parity to Atlas. Available in Community at no additional cost under the SSPL; mongot released under SSPL. Community: Linux, Docker, Kubernetes Operator; Enterprise self-managed deployed on Kubernetes via MongoDB Controllers for Kubernetes. Automated Embedding still public preview on Community.
