Search-tool summary: LangSmith has three main deployment models (Cloud, BYOC, Self-hosted). Self-hosted and BYOC both require the Enterprise plan. BYOC: control plane in LangChain's cloud, data plane runs entirely in customer cloud; LangChain provisions and operates; GA announcement says BYOC available for Enterprise customers across 15 AWS regions in the US, EU and APAC. Self-hosted: LangChain ships container images, Helm charts and licence; customer provides Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Redis; docs position it for data residency, regulatory constraints, custom networking or air-gapped requirements. Kubernetes minimum at least 16 vCPUs, 64GB memory; datastores PostgreSQL, Redis, ClickHouse, optional blob storage. Hybrid: LangChain hosts control plane, customer runs Agent Servers / data plane.
