Phoenix (open source): free to self-host; no Phoenix feature gated by the commercial offering.
AX Free ($0): 25k spans per month, 1 GB ingestion per month, 15 days retention; Signal capped at 10 issues/mo; no credit card.
AX Pro ($50/month): 50k spans per month, 10 GB ingestion per month, 30 days retention; Signal capped at 25 issues/mo; overages rather than hard stop.
AX Enterprise (custom): span volume, storage and retention negotiated; adds ADB Data Fabric, VPC / on-prem deployment, HIPAA, unlimited Signal, custom managed agents, repo access, support SLAs.
Prices on span and data volume, with unlimited users and unlimited evaluations on every hosted tier; no per-seat charges.
