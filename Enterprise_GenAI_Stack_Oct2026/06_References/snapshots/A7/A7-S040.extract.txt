OpenSSF blog, 4 April 2025: the AI/ML Working Group launched version 1.0 of the model-signing project, with contributors from Google, NVIDIA and HiddenLayer. Library and CLI "for signing and verification of ML models, supporting any type of model format and models of any size"; works with Sigstore, self-signed certificates or plain key pairs while maintaining the same hashing scheme and signature format (a sigstore bundle). Formalised as the OMS specification (github.com/ossf/model-signing-spec): detached signature file typically distributed within the model folder.
July 2026 OpenSSF podcast (episode 65, Mihai Maruseac) refers to follow-on iterations v1.1 and v1.2.
Commentary (HackerNoon, secondary): OMS confirms integrity and signer, not provenance.
