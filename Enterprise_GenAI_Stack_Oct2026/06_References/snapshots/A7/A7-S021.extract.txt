Cisco announced its intent to acquire Robust Intelligence in August 2024; terms not disclosed. Cisco's announcement page carries a September 24, 2024 update saying the deal was completed (a Cisco product page and a third-party profile say October 2024). Cisco product page: Robust Intelligence "has been foundational to the development of Cisco AI Defense and Cisco Foundation AI".
