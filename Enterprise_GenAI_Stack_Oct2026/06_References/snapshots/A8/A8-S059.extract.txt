- 30 May 2024 statement gives initial guidance to firms using AI when providing investment services to retail clients, grounded in MiFID II organisational, conduct and best-interest obligations.
- "Firms' decisions remain the responsibility of management bodies, irrespective of whether those decisions are taken by people or AI-based tools."
- Firms should "establish robust governance structures, conduct regular AI model testing, and monitor AI systems"; ex-ante controls on accuracy of information supplied to AI systems; "sufficiently frequent ex-post controls".
- Third-party AI solutions: due diligence and controls (outsourcing). Chatbots: "transparently disclose to clients the use of such technology". Heightened diligence for advice and portfolio management (suitability).
- Feb 2026 algorithmic trading briefing: MiFID II/RTS 6 do not specifically address AI; reliance on external technology providers does not alter regulatory obligations.
- Feb 2026 joint consultation treats understanding of AI as essential for management body governance.
- No standalone 2025/2026 ESMA statement on AI for UCITS/AIF managers found.
