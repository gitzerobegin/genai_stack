- 19 June 2025: Royal Assent; some provisions in force automatically (s.142).
- 20 August 2025 (Commencement No. 1, SI 2025/904): Part 1 smart data, parts of Part 5 amending UK GDPR, DPA 2018 and PECR, and Part 6 Information Commission provisions.
- 5 February 2026 (Commencement No. 6, SI 2026/82): the majority of the Part 5 data protection and privacy provisions, including research definitions (s.67), purpose limitation (s.71), automated decision-making (s.80), third-country transfers (s.85), and Schedule 12 (cookies/terminal equipment).
- 19 June 2026: section 103 (complaints by data subjects) and Schedule 10 in force; new controller complaint duties apply to complaints submitted on or after that date.
- ICO guidance updated 19 June 2026: all data protection provisions in the DUAA are now in force; changes phased in between June 2025 and June 2026.
