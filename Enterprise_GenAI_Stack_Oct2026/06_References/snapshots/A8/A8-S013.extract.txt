Search-tool extract:
- The Omnibus did not postpone Article 4, which has applied since 2 February 2025. Amended Article 4 took effect on entry into force of the Omnibus, 27 July 2026.
- Original text required providers and deployers to "ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level" of AI literacy; amended text requires them to take measures to support the development of AI literacy (obligation of means).
- New clarification: the obligation does not oblige providers or deployers to guarantee any specific literacy level for any individual.
- Commission and Member States must support and facilitate efforts of providers and deployers, particularly SMEs.
- The Commission originally proposed removing the binding obligation; the final text kept a lighter duty.
- Council formally adopted 29 June; European Parliament voted 16 June [2026].
