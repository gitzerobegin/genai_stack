- "The implementation date for final rules and policy materials reflecting policy changes set out in this PS is 18 March 2027." (PRA PS7/26); FCA PS26/2 rules also start 18 March 2027.
- Incident reporting: one report via FCA Connect in three phases; initial within 24 hours of determining threshold met; final within 30 working days of resolution.
- Material third-party (MTP) arrangements: firms notify before entering into or significantly changing an MTP arrangement; via FCA Connect; not an approval process; annual register of third-party arrangements (90 calendar days to submit once window opens).
- Updated SS1/26 (incident reporting) and revised SS2/21 (outsourcing and third-party risk) effective from 18 March 2027, published as part of PS7/26.
- CP17/24 (December 2024) was the consultation.
