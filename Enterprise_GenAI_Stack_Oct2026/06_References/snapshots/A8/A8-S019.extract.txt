- Article 51: GPAI model with systemic risk if among the most advanced or equivalent impact; presumption where cumulative training compute greater than 10^25 FLOP; Commission may designate.
- Article 53 (all GPAI providers): technical documentation for AI Office and authorities; information to downstream providers; copyright compliance policy; publish sufficiently detailed summary of training content using Commission template. Open-source exemption from documentation duties does not apply to models with systemic risk.
- Article 55 (systemic risk): model evaluation with standardised protocols including adversarial testing; assess and mitigate systemic risks; report serious incidents; ensure cybersecurity.
- Obligations applied from 2 August 2025. In first year AI Office works closely with providers. From 2 August 2026 Commission can enforce: request information, request model access for evaluations, require risk mitigation, fines up to 3% of global annual turnover, or require restriction/withdrawal/recall.
- "Providers of general-purpose AI models that have been placed on the market before 2 August 2025 shall take the necessary steps in order to comply with the obligations laid down in this Regulation by 2 August 2027."
