- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 is the "Digital Omnibus on AI". It amends the AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), along with Regulations 2018/1139 and 2023/1230, to simplify implementation of harmonised AI rules. Adopted 8 July 2026; Official Journal L series 24 July 2026.
- Enters into force on the third day after publication in the Official Journal [27 July 2026].
- Rationale: late standards, common specifications and guidance, plus delayed national authorities, made the original 2 August 2026 start date for high-risk obligations unworkable.
- Article 4 (AI literacy) amended: providers and deployers to take measures [to support AI literacy of staff].
- Article 43(3) technical correction; notified bodies may assess high-risk systems for 18 months from 27 July 2026 under specified conditions.
- Articles 57, 58, 60 amended (sandboxes; real-world testing for certain Annex I high-risk systems).
- The Commission's power to adopt a harmonised template for post-market monitoring plans under Article 72(3) is removed.
- Providers that placed generative systems on the market before 2 August 2026 receive a transition [to 2 December 2026] for Article 50(2) marking duties.
- New application dates: 2 December 2027 for Annex III high-risk systems; 2 August 2028 for Annex I high-risk systems; Articles 102 to 110 apply from 27 July 2026.
- Council corrigendum dated 22 September 2026 (ST-13206-2026) fixes German text.
