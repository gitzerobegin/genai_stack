Search-tool extract:
- "This SS is effective from 17 May 2024 and is published as part of PS6/23." Policy published 17 May 2023.
- Five principles: Principle 1 – Model identification and model risk classification; Principle 2 – Governance; Principle 3 – Model development, implementation and use; Principle 4 – Independent model validation; Principle 5 – Model risk mitigants.
- Sub-principles include proportionate implementation, allocating responsibility for the MRM framework to a Senior Management Function holder, reporting MRM effectiveness to the audit committee, and managing risks from AI techniques such as machine learning.
- "The principles are applicable to all types of models that are used to inform business decisions, whether developed in-house or externally (including vendor models)."
- Scope: banks, building societies, and PRA-designated investment firms that hold internal model approval for credit risk, market risk, or counterparty credit risk capital. Insurers are not covered.
- PRA describes the principles as technology-agnostic and outcomes-focussed but deliberately included factors relevant to the use of AI models; explainability and transparency named as factors when judging model complexity; banks expected to keep a complete model inventory including AI/ML models (BoE DSIT/HMT letter 2024).
- The PRA treats these as expectations; they complement existing supervisory expectations for selected model types.
