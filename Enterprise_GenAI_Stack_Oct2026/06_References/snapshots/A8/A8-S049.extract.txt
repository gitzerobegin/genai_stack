Search-tool extract:
- SYSC 8.1: "an operational function is regarded as critical or important if a defect or failure in its performance would materially impair the continuing compliance of a common platform firm" [with conditions of authorisation/obligations], or its financial performance or continuity of investment services.
- Excluded functions include advisory, legal advice, staff training, billing, premises and personnel security, and standardised services such as market information and price feeds.
- "the outsourcing must not result in the delegation by senior management of their responsibility"; care and competence through to termination (SYSC 8.1.7); provider capacity, ongoing performance review, in-house expertise to supervise (SYSC 8.1.8).
- "the firm must be able to terminate the arrangement for the outsourcing where necessary, with immediate effect when this is in the interest of its clients"; regulator and auditors have effective access to data and premises.
- Sub-outsourcing "only takes place with the firm's consent in writing" (SYSC 8.1.9). Notification of critical, important or material outsourcing (SYSC 8.1.12, SYSC 13.9.2). Common platform provisions seen dated 23/10/2025.
- FG16/5: page last updated 18/03/2026 (adding link to new rules on reporting material third party arrangements); guidance PDF July 2016 (updated September 2019). Not binding. Cloud for important business functions is a form of (potentially material) outsourcing. Covers data location, effective access to data, concentration risk, supply chain and sub-contracting, exit plans. Firms subject to EBA outsourcing guidelines are excluded from FG16/5 scope.
- FCA new notification regime for material third-party arrangements applies from 18 March 2027.
