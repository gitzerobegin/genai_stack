- 2 February 2025: prohibited-practice rules and AI literacy duties began to apply. Commission has published guidelines on prohibitions.
- 2 August 2025: governance rules and obligations for general-purpose AI models began to apply.
- 2 August 2026: transparency duties under Article 50 and enforcement: "From 2 August 2026, the AI Office and authorities of the Member States are responsible for implementing, supervising and enforcing the AI Act. The AI Office can request information, request access to a model for evaluations, require risk mitigation measures, and issue fines of up to 3% of global annual turnover."
- Providers of generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 for Article 50(2) marking/detection (earlier Commission proposal suggested 2 February 2027).
- New prohibitions on generating non-consensual intimate material and CSAM start 2 December 2026.
- High-risk Annex III moved from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027 ("CEN and CENELEC have not been able to develop the standards in the requested timeline"); Annex I to 2 August 2028. Legacy public-sector high-risk systems: 2 August 2030.
- Political agreement 7 May 2026; omnibus entered into force 27 July 2026.
- Caveat: some Commission pages still describe the older schedule.
