Search-tool extract:
- "The EU regulation entered into force on 16 January 2023 and will apply as of 17 January 2025." Covers more than 20 different types of financial entities (ESMA).
- Register of information: from application date, all in-scope financial entities need a comprehensive register of contractual arrangements with ICT third-party service providers at entity, sub-consolidated and consolidated levels; lets firms monitor ICT third-party risk, authorities supervise, and ESAs designate CTPPs.
- Competent authorities to report registers by 30 April 2025 (first round). FAQ: "the deadline for competent authorities to report the registers to the ESAs is set to 31 March of each calendar year from 2026 onwards"; reference date 31 December of preceding year from 2026. Plain CSV format.
- DORA pillars: ICT risk management, ICT-related incidents, resilience testing, ICT third-party risk management, information sharing, oversight of critical third-party providers.
