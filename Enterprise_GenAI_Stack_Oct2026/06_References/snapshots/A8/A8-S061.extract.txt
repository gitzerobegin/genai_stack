- "firms should adopt the following definition of a model as the basis for determining the scope of their MRM frameworks": a quantitative method, system, or approach that applies statistical, economic, financial, or mathematical theories [remainder of sentence cut off in search snippet].
- "Quantitative methods" are the wider category (calculation methods, systems, approaches, end-user computing applications, calculators); "models are a subset of quantitative methods".
- Principle 1.1(b), "notwithstanding" the definition: material deterministic quantitative methods such as decision-based rules or algorithms that are not classified as a model, have a material bearing on business decisions and are complex in nature - relevant aspects of the MRM framework can be applied.
- PS6/23 feedback: respondents concerned a broad reading of 1.1(b) could bring non-model approaches into scope; PRA gave examples of highly complex quantitative calculation systems (list cut off after "Electronic Trading").
