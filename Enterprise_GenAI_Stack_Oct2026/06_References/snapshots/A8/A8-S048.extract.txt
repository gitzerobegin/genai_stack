- SS2/21 Chapter 10: documented exit plan "for material outsourcing arrangements where such an exit is considered possible, explicitly catering for the unexpected termination of an outsourcing agreement".
- Stressed exit: "last resort risk mitigation strategy when disruption cannot be managed through other business continuity measures, such as the insolvency or liquidation of a service provider"; focus on outcome (continued provision of important business services) not method; routes include bringing in-house or transferring to an alternative/back-up provider.
- "Firms should begin to develop their business continuity and exit plans, in particular for stressed exits, during the pre-outsourcing phase once they have determined that a planned outsourcing arrangement is material." Plans tested as soon as practically possible.
- "Firms should actively consider temporary measures that can help ensure the ongoing provision of important business services following a disruption and/or a stressed exit".
