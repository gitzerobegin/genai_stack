Search tool summary (Italian, translated):
- 28 January 2025: Garante requested information from the two Chinese companies behind DeepSeek (what data, sources, purposes, legal basis, whether stored on servers in China); 20 days to answer.
- 30 January 2025: urgent measure with immediate effect limiting processing of Italian users' data; investigation opened. The companies said they do not operate in Italy and European law does not apply to them; the Garante rejected this.
- Provvedimento 10098477 finds violations of Art. 31 (cooperation), Art. 6/information duties (privacy notice lacks legal bases), Chapter III (data-subject rights), Art. 32 (security) given data stored in the People's Republic of China. Operative text uses "limitazione definitiva" under Art. 58(2)(f), while reserving further determinations; a Garante member described it as essentially precautionary.
- Garante member Agostino Ghiglia: first measure of its kind worldwide. 2025 annual report cites it as first action of the year.
- No source found indicating the limitation was lifted or that a fine concluded the investigation.
