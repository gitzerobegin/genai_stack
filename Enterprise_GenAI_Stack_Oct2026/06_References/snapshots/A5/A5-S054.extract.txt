Search tool summary:
- Secretary of Home Affairs issued PSPF Direction 001-2025, dated 4 February 2025 (page updated 14 February 2025).
- Government entities must prevent access, use or installation of DeepSeek products, applications and web services and remove existing instances from government systems and devices.
- Based on threat and risk analysis including cyber security. Mandatory for all non-corporate Commonwealth entities; applies to personnel accessing Australian Government information. Does not restrict private individuals or businesses. Similar direction issued for Kaspersky.
