Search tool summary:
- February 2025: PIPC analysis found third-party data transfers and an inadequate privacy policy; recommended temporary suspension; DeepSeek removed its app from Korean App Store and Google Play on 15 February 2025.
- 24 April 2025 findings: DeepSeek transferred Korean users' data abroad without consent to four overseas companies (three in China, one in the US) until downloads were suspended; data included AI prompt content and device, network and app information; privacy policy omitted legally required items. PIPC recommended corrective measures including deletion of transferred data. PIPC identified recipient Beijing Volcano Engine Technology as a ByteDance affiliate but a separate legal entity.
- DeepSeek submitted a revised Korean privacy policy; reinstatement in Korean app stores reported only by a secondary site (unverified).
