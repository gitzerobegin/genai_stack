- CAISI evaluated the open-weight DeepSeek V4 Pro in April 2026 and published 1 May 2026.
- CAISI evaluations indicate DeepSeek V4's capabilities lag the frontier by about 8 months; most capable PRC model CAISI had evaluated to date.
