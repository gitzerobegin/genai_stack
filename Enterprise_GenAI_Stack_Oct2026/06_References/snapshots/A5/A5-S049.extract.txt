Search tool summary:
- CAISI published 30 September 2025 an evaluation of PRC developer DeepSeek's models (R1, R1-0528, V3.1) vs US models (GPT-5, GPT-5-mini, gpt-oss, Opus 4) across 19 benchmarks; DeepSeek lags in performance, cost, security and adoption.
- Best US model solves 20-80% more software-engineering and cyber tasks; one US reference model cost 35% less on average at similar performance.
- DeepSeek models much more likely to follow malicious agent-hijacking instructions; highly susceptible to jailbreaking; assisted with a majority of malicious requests evaluated.
- Report states security shortcomings and censorship of model responses may pose risks to application developers, consumers and US national security.
- Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick used the report to argue against reliance on foreign AI.
