- Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection Meike Kamp reported DeepSeek to Apple and Google as "illegal content" under Article 16 of the Digital Services Act (27 June 2025), coordinated with Baden-Württemberg, Rhineland-Palatinate, Bremen regulators and the Federal Network Agency.
- Grounds: DeepSeek transfers German users' data to China without adequate safeguards; no convincing evidence of EU-equivalent protection; Chinese government access rights. DeepSeek's privacy policy says it stores requests and uploaded files on computers in China.
- 6 May 2025 request to DeepSeek to comply or withdraw; DeepSeek did not comply. A fine was judged unenforceable in China.
- France, Netherlands, Luxembourg and Portugal also examining DeepSeek. Outcome of the app-store request not found.
