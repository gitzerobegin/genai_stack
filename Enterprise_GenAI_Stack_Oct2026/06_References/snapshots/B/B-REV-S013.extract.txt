"Teams and Roles on the Cohere Platform" (docs.cohere.com), with cohere.com/enterprise-data-commitments and North product pages, as summarised by the search tool:
- The standard Cohere platform has two team roles, Owner and User; owners invite members from the Team page of the Dashboard.
- No SAML/SSO configuration guide, identity-provider list or finer-grained dashboard RBAC was found for the hosted API platform.
- Enterprise data commitments: log data (organisational ID, dates of actions) is generated only on the SaaS Platform; Cohere does not receive log data in other deployment types.
- North (Cohere's separate enterprise agent platform) advertises integration with existing IAM, roles and permissions and audit-ready logs; this is a different product from the Embed/Rerank API.
