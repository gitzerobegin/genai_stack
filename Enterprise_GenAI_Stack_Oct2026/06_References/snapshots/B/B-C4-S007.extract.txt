Okta help and blog pages (Add AI agents manually; Configure imported AI agents; Update AI agents; blog "Resource Access Certifications for AI Agents"), as reported by the search tool:
- AI agents are managed under Directory > AI Agents in the Admin Console; manual registration requires a profile, platform identifier and external ID that keeps each agent unique.
- Discovered agents are registered into Universal Directory alongside workforce users, with explicit human ownership, business purpose and cryptographic credentials.
- Owners can be individual admins (up to five) or a group (at least two members); Okta recommends at least two owners.
- Identity Governance > Access Certifications supports campaigns with "AI Agents" as identity type and resource owners as reviewers.
- System Log: only a training page states an agent's identifier can be used to locate its activity in the Okta System Log (not reference documentation).
