Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Pricing page (Memory section), as summarised by the search tool:
- Short-term memory billed on ingestion, storage until TTL, and retrieval. From 6 October 2026: ingestion US$1.00 per GB, retrieval US$0.20 per GB, storage US$0.10 per GB-month (previously US$0.25 per 1,000 new events until 5 October 2026). Events billed at a 12 KB minimum and 64 KB maximum for ingestion and retrieval.
- Long-term memory: storage US$0.75 per 1,000 records per month (built-in strategies) or US$0.25 per 1,000 records per month (built-in overrides or self-managed strategies); retrieval US$0.50 per 1,000 retrievals.
- Worked example: about US$18.97 per month for 100,000 short-term events, 10,000 long-term records and 20,000 retrievals.
- Harness-managed memory incurs standard AgentCore Memory charges. Billing of the newer IngestData direct-to-long-term path not stated.
