ICO guidance "Principle (e): Storage limitation" (UK GDPR), as reported by the search tool:
- Personal data must be kept for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.
- "You should also periodically review the data you hold, and erase or anonymise it when you no longer need it."
- If it is appropriate to delete personal data from a live system, you should also delete it from any back-up of the information on that system.
