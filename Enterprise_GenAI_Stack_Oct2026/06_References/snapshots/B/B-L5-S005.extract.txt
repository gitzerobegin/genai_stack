ICO guidance "Right to erasure" (UK GDPR), as reported by the search tool:
- If a valid request is received and no exemption applies, the controller must take steps to erase from backup systems as well as live systems.
- Erasure can be fulfilled instantly in live systems while data may remain in backups until overwritten; the key issue is to put backup data "beyond use" (not used for any other purpose, held until replaced on an established schedule).
- Controllers must be clear with individuals about what will happen to their data, including in backups.
- Respond without undue delay and within one month of receipt (extension possible in some circumstances).
- The page states that, due to changes made by the Data (Use and Access) Act, the guidance is under review and may be subject to change.
