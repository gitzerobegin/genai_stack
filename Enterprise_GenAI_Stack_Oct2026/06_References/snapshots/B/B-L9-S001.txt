Datadog's Response to the npm Supply Chain Worm (keyv / jaredwray Maintainer Account Compromise)
Incidents
We are aware of a supply chain attack in which the npm maintainer account for 
jaredwray
 was compromised, resulting in ~1800+ malicious 
compromised package versions
 being published beginning August 4, 2026.

Upon learning of this activity, Datadog's security team investigated potential exposure across our products, public packages, customer-facing infrastructure, and internal development environments. One Datadog-published npm package, 
backstage-plugin-datadog-entity-sync-backend
 (versions 1.1.1, 1.1.2, 1.1.4, and 1.1.5), referenced a compromised package as a transitive dependency. We confirmed that Datadog was not impacted. As a precaution, we have blocked all known malicious package versions across our internal systems and continue to monitor for exposure.

This supply chain attack campaign involved a self-propagating worm that has continued to spread to maintainer accounts and packages beyond the initial set. We will update this post if the scope or our exposure assessment changes. We recommend checking the 
compromised package versions
 against your own dependencies. If you use the affected versions listed, review the potentially affected systems for the campaign's 
indicators of compromise
.

For a detailed breakdown of the campaign and how to assess impact in your own environment, see our 
Security Labs post
 for the current authoritative list.

If you have any further questions, our 
Support team
 is here to help.
Datadog's Response to Vector Security Vulnerabilities (GHSA-rrfg-9487-mhp6, GHSA-qp6f-fpfx-4gg6, GHSA-6342-xwvw-c637)
Vulnerabilities
Summary

Datadog's security team was notified on June 18, 2026 of three vulnerabilities in Vector, the open-source data pipeline tool, reported by DeMarcus Campbell, Information Security at International Exchange, Inc. (ICE), through coordinated disclosure. Patches for all three issues are available in Vector v0.57.0, released on July 14, 2026. Our investigation found no evidence that these vulnerabilities were exploited in the wild or that any Datadog customer data was accessed or exfiltrated.

Cyber Essentials Plus (CE+) certification
Compliance
We are pleased to announce that Datadog has achieved its first Cyber Essentials Plus (CE+) certification. CE+ is a UK Government-backed certification that independently verifies an organization's protections against common cyber threats.

The certification covers Datadog's UK operations, including the UK production environment, UK-based employees, their end-user devices and associated supporting infrastructure. All other Datadog networks, locations, and infrastructure are outside the certification scope.

Datadog Mobile Application - Android Vulnerabilities
Vulnerabilities
Summary:
 Datadog was notified about 6 low severity vulnerabilities on the Datadog Android Application on April 17, 2026 by Mark Esler (
https://github.com/eslerm
). These vulnerabilities were patched in v545-5.9.2, released on April 23, 2026. Our investigation found no evidence that these vulnerabilities were exploited in the wild or that any Datadog customer data was accessed or exfiltrated. CVE IDs for these vulnerabilities are pending and will be included in a follow-up post once assigned. We’ve included a summary of each vulnerability below:

Vulnerabilities:

Exported OnCallNotificationActivity allows cross-app Intent launch and spoofed on-call page acknowledgement

Exported widget configuration activities render authenticated infrastructure data for sequentially-enumerable appWidgetId values

Exported BubbleChatActivity allows a co-installed application to dismiss the legitimate Bits AI chat notification

Datadog Android application stores monitoring metadata and search history in unencrypted SQLite databases

Exported AppActivity accepts attacker-supplied OAuth session from Intent extras, enabling account confusion

Firebase Crashlytics setUserId propagates the Datadog user UUID to Google without a visible consent gate

If you have any questions, reach out via your Customer Success Manager, Account Executive, or through 
Datadog Support
.
