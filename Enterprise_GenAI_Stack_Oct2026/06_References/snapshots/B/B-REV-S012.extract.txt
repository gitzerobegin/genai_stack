"Set up user access to the console (federated)" and Workforce Identity Federation pages, as summarised by the search tool:
- Workforce Identity Federation lets users reach Google Cloud with their existing IdP over OIDC or SAML 2.0; multiple IdPs per pool (Okta, Ping, AD FS, Microsoft Entra ID).
