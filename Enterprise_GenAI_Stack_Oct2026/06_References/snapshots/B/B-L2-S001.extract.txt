Red Hat AI Inference 3.4 release notes (search-tool extract): In version 3.4, Red Hat AI Inference Server (RHAIIS) is now called Red Hat AI Inference, and its scope expanded to include distributed inference. The standalone vLLM-based inference experience is preserved and continues as a supported deployment option.
Related Red Hat product page (https://www.redhat.com/en/products/ai/inference): llm-d-based capabilities are included in Red Hat AI Enterprise and Red Hat OpenShift AI; third-party Kubernetes support described as early access.
