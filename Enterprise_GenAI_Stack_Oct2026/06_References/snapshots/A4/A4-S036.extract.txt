========================================================================

Plus: $39 per seat per month, up to 10k base traces / mo, then pay-as-you-go, access to Deployment and Engine. Enterprise: custom pricing, invoiced annually upfront. Plus plans include 1 free small serverless deployment; additional serverless or dedicated deployments charged on usage (resource time). Cloud deployments require Plus or above. Self-hosted deployment with a control plane requires the Enterprise plan with LangSmith Deployment enabled. Billing docs (docs.langchain.com/langsmith/billing): deployed agents are charged via LangSmith Deployment, billed on resources consumed, compute measured in LangChain Standard Units (LSU) and storage in LSU; this usage-based model replaces the previous per-run and uptime pricing; existing customers remain on current pricing until October 1, 2026. Pricing page: 1 LSU costs $1 (shown in Engine section).
