FOUND VIA: vLLM PyTorch Foundation hosted project joins
========================================================================

PyTorch Foundation expanded into an umbrella foundation on May 7, 2025; vLLM and DeepSpeed accepted as foundation-hosted projects by the Technical Advisory Council. Foundation-hosted projects adopt the foundation's open governance model and receive operational support (distinct from independently governed ecosystem projects; vLLM had earlier been a PyTorch ecosystem project). April 2026 Helion announcement lists foundation-hosted projects DeepSpeed, PyTorch, Ray and vLLM. Ray joined as hosted project (Sept/Oct 2025, sources differ).
