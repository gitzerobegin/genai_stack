Self-hosted with control plane: you run the Deployment control plane and Agent Servers in your own Kubernetes cluster, next to self-hosted LangSmith; requires the Enterprise plan with LangSmith Deployment enabled. Hybrid: LangChain runs the control plane, while Agent Servers and their data plane sit in your infrastructure; traces flow to LangSmith Cloud or self-hosted LangSmith. BYOC: data stays in your VPC while LangChain manages the infrastructure. Self-hosted and BYOC are available on the Enterprise plan.
