- Portfolio tier: AWS applying guide and benefits pages give up to US$200,000 in credits for provider-backed start-ups. AWS's "everything you need to know" article says US$100,000 on one aws.amazon.com version and US$200,000 on the startups.aws.com copy (AWS materials inconsistent).
- Additional AWS credits above US$200,000 are available for AI start-ups ready to scale beyond Activate's tiers.
- Portfolio eligibility: affiliation with an Activate Provider (VC, accelerator, incubator) and its Organization ID; pre-Series B (most recent funding round within the last 12 months, if applicable); working company website; founded within the past 10 years.
- Founders tier (no affiliation): US$1,000 in Activate credits; select start-ups may qualify for up to US$5,000 over time; requires an AWS account on a Paid Tier plan.
- Credits usually expire within 1-2 years depending on the package.
