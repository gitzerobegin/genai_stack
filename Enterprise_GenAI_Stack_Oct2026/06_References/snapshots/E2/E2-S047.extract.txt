- 20 August 2025: CSA released the official mapping of the AI Controls Matrix (AICM v1.0) to ISO/IEC 42001:2023, control by control, with pointers to ISO/IEC 27001/27002 for compensating controls.
- 23 October 2025: CSA launched STAR for AI. Level 1 = self-assessment: publish an AI-CAIQ (v1.0.2) self-assessment to the STAR Registry aligned with AICM. Level 2 = third-party ISO/IEC 42001 certification uploaded to the registry plus a "Valid-AI-ted" scored AI-CAIQ.
- Valid-AI-ted is CSA's automated scoring engine for AI-CAIQ self-assessments; a passing Level 1 earns an enhanced designation.
- The October 2025 press release said Level 2 would launch in November 2025; the STAR for AI page now lists Level 2 as available.
- 14 July 2026 CSA blog: AICM v1.1 described as an implementation bridge for ISO/IEC 42001 (status details not verified).
