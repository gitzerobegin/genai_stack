Search tool summary (results: docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/buyerguide/buyer-private-offers.html; docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/creating-private-offer.html; What's New 2017-11, 2023-10, 2024-05; AWS Marketplace blog on private offers best practice; third-party oneuptime.com, clazar.io not used).
- Buyer guide: a private offer is tied to the buyer account(s) named in it; one offer can cover up to 25 accounts; accepting creates an agreement with the seller.
- Accepting from an AWS Organizations management account can extend terms to member accounts; for AMI and container products the licence can also be shared via AWS License Manager; member accounts not previously subscribed must accept the private offer to deploy.
- Seller guide: call-to-action buttons (request private offer / demo) on product pages for AMI, SaaS, container and CloudFormation products require membership of the APN Customer Engagements (ACE) programme.
- Sellers view and manage private-offer agreements in the AWS Marketplace Management Portal for AMI, container, SaaS and professional services products (What's New, May 2024).
- Sellers can issue a new private offer to change an existing SaaS contract (new entitlements, discounts, payment schedules, standardised EULA).
- Seller private offers announced November 2017.
