- AWS: Bedrock offers four service tiers for model inference, Reserved, Priority, Standard and Flex, chosen per request.
- AWS pricing page: Priority tier pricing is at a 75% premium to Standard tier pricing; Flex is at a 50% discount to Standard (supported models only).
- AWS: select foundation models from providers including Anthropic, Meta, Mistral AI and Amazon are available for batch inference at a 50% lower price than on-demand inference.
- Reserved tier: capacity bought per tokens-per-minute for a 1- or 3-month term; minimums 100,000 input TPM and 10,000 output TPM; arranged through the AWS account team; overflow goes to Standard; cache writes count toward reserved TPM.
- Prompt caching "up to 90%" input saving appears only in third-party sources in these results (not confirmed on AWS pages).
- Provisioned Throughput billed hourly per model unit; a third-party analysis notes hourly rates are published only for some older models.
