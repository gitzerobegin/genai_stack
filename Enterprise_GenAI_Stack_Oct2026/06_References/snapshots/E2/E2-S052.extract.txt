- The service provider creates an endpoint service and grants customers access; the service consumer creates an interface VPC endpoint to reach it.
- A SaaS provider can use a Network Load Balancer targeting instances in its VPC as the endpoint service; customers in AWS are then granted access.
- Best practice: create a PrivateLink endpoint in each Availability Zone in the region where the service is deployed.
- SaaS products powered by AWS PrivateLink can be discovered, purchased and provisioned through AWS Marketplace.
