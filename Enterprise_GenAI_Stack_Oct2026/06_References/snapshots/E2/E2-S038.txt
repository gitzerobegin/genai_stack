Google Cloud Marketplace private offer enhancements unlock enterprise and AI use cases
October 16, 2024
Sakshi Goel
Group Product Manager, Google Cloud
Steve Holden
Business Development Manager, Google Cloud Marketplace

When it comes to purchasing technology for different departments and business units that operate across the globe, enterprise customers need flexibility and choice. This needs to extend to the technology, including generative AI solutions, that integrate with a customer’s Google Cloud environment and that are procured through Google Cloud Marketplace.

Google Cloud Marketplace 
offers a high-quality catalog of innovative ISV solutions, provides flexible ways to try and buy them, and maximizes the value of cloud investments by allowing purchases of ISV solutions to draw down on Google Cloud commitments. 
By 
bringing much of the traditional IT sales process online, we help streamline transactions between customers, technology vendors, and 
channel partners
, whether through public listings or through custom, negotiated private offers. 

Private offers are an effective way for customers and partners to align on terms and payment structures that match an enterprise’s specific needs. Today, we are excited to announce additional private offer enhancements, and the enterprise use cases they unlock.

Support for enterprise AI purchasing models

Google Cloud Marketplace supports the buying and selling of generative AI SaaS solutions and key foundational models that are deployable to Vertex AI. These ground-breaking solutions are helping enterprises deliver innovative business applications ranging from high-quality code generation and summarizing large documents to generating content for products and services. Google Cloud Marketplace private offers make it possible for customers to transact third-party foundational models and LLMs through a variety of transaction models including committed use discounts (CUDs), usage-based discounts, and, now, 
provisioned throughput
, a fixed-cost subscription service that reserves throughput for supported generative AI models on Vertex AI. 

We designed provisioned throughput to provide customers and partners the flexibility to transact and use any model from a partner-specified model family based on the capacity purchased. Provisioned throughput is suitable for customers that are building real-time generative AI applications such as chatbots and customer agents; enabling critical workloads that require consistently high throughput; and maintaining deterministic costs with control of overages. At the same time, customers can continue to take advantage of the spending optimization that procuring through Google Cloud Marketplace provides, such as the ability to draw down on Google Cloud commitments by spending on ISV solutions, including generative AI models.
Tailor offers and payment schedules with multiple orders

When purchasing an ISV’s SaaS solution on Google Cloud Marketplace, each individual business unit within an enterprise now has the flexibility to get different subscriptions and payment schedules based on their cost center’s needs. This capability allows customers to have multiple orders for the same product, which is particularly useful for large enterprises with different departments, subsidiaries, or international branches. Furthermore, each unit can have customized subscription plans for the same technology solution. This feature is generally available for fixed-fee SaaS solutions via private offer today. To enable this, partners can turn on multiple orders for their applicable products in Google Cloud Marketplace. Check out a video demo of how this works.
Customers and ISV partners are already enabling enterprise use cases and seeing the value this new feature unlocks.

Partners can also take advantage of 
simplified payment schedules for ISV-directed and reseller-initiated private offers
 to better deliver flexible payment options to end-customers. Google Cloud Marketplace private offers support monthly, quarterly, annual, and custom installment payment schedules, including prorated payments. This enhancement simplifies the user experience for managing variable payment schedules.

Upfront payments and extended contract lengths

In addition, customers have improved flexibility over how they manage their spend by making upfront payments for critical technology solutions up to five years in advance. The ability to make 
multi-year upfront payments
 is available for private offers and lets customers draw down on their Google Cloud commitment on an annual amortized basis. Furthermore, we are also introducing contract lengths of up to 7 years. These capabilities provide more flexible spend management for customers and improved control over financials for our partners.
