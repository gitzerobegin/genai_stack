- Ownership: as between Customer and OpenAI, and to the extent permitted by law, Customer retains all ownership rights in Input and owns all Output; OpenAI assigns to Customer its right, title and interest (if any) in Output.
- Customer Content use: OpenAI will only use Customer Content as necessary to provide the Services, comply with applicable law, enforce the OpenAI Policies and prevent abuse. A government-reseller copy adds that OpenAI will not use it to train models except for fine-tuning, embeddings and similar activities solely for the Customer's benefit.
- Competing models: restriction (e) bars using Output to develop models that compete with OpenAI, except for a Permitted Exception. Older business terms describe the carve-out as models primarily intended to categorise, classify or organise data (e.g. embeddings or classifiers) not distributed or made commercially available to third parties, plus fine-tuning models provided as part of the Services.
- Resale: no general resale ban found in the results; the closest provision prohibits buying, selling or transferring API keys to or with a third party.
