Search tool summary (results: openai.com/api-reserved-tier/ and localised copies; third-party blog codex.danielvaughan.com).
- Reserved Tier is the current product; the older Scale Tier still covers models released before GPT-5.6. Available to Enterprise customers via sales.
- Pre-purchased throughput that sits on top of rate limits and stays available during peak demand.
- Bought per model, measured in dollars per minute; can be spent across Standard and Fast modes, context lengths and regions.
- Batch and Flex processing are not covered.
- Usage above the reservation billed at pay-as-you-go rates (e.g. a US$2/minute reservation covers any minute with eligible usage of US$2 or less).
- A third-party blog describes a "Guaranteed Capacity" programme (May 2026, 1-3 year commitments, tiered discounts): unconfirmed.
