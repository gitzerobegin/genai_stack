MongoDB announced the Voyage AI deal on February 24, 2025 (investors.mongodb.com press release). MongoDB's SEC filings give the acquisition date as February 17, 2025. The 10-K describes buying all outstanding shares of Voyage AI Innovations, Inc.; purchase price $160.9 million, made up of 484,169 shares of common stock valued at $141.4 million and $19.5 million in cash.
