Cohere's changelog and newsroom date the Embed 5 launch to September 30, 2026. Tiers: Embed 5 Pro (embed-v5.0-pro) and Embed 5 Fast (embed-v5.0-fast), shared embedding space; multimodal; 100+ languages; 128K context; Matryoshka 256-2048 dims; float/int8/binary. Pricing: text $0.12 per million tokens Pro, $0.08 Fast; image input $0.40 per million tokens. Available via Cohere API, Microsoft Foundry, Amazon SageMaker, Model Vault; self-hostable with vLLM for private deployments.
