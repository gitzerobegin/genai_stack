OpenAI's deprecations page lists June 3, 2026 as the announcement date and November 30, 2026 as the scheduled shutdown for Agent Builder. AgentKit announcement: the product will no longer be available on the OpenAI platform from November 30, 2026. ChatKit remains available. Recommended replacements: the Agents SDK for workflows that should live as code; Workspace Agents in ChatGPT for natural-language use cases. Evals dashboard and API also shut down November 30, 2026 (existing evals read-only from October 31); v1/prompts API and reusable prompt objects share the November 30 date.
