GA December 2025: a single index can hold up to 2 billion vectors, with up to 10,000 indexes per vector bucket (40x the 50 million per index in preview). Limitations page: "Vectors per vector index: Up to 2 billion." A GA blog subheading cites 1 billion but its body and the re:Invent recap say 2 billion. Pricing dimensions: PUT per logical GB, storage per logical GB, queries per-API plus $/TB by index size (lower beyond 100,000 vectors); later post: query charges on indexes >10 million vectors reduced by up to 80%. Bedrock-related AWS blog example storage rate $0.06 per GB per month. March 2026 expansion added 17 Regions: "S3 Vectors is now available in 31 AWS Regions."
