Announcement dated May 7, 2025 (PyTorch Day France, Paris): the PyTorch Foundation expanded into an umbrella foundation; the Technical Advisory Council accepted vLLM and DeepSpeed as foundation-hosted projects. Foundation now hosts PyTorch, vLLM, DeepSpeed, Ray, Helion (April 2026) and Safetensors.
