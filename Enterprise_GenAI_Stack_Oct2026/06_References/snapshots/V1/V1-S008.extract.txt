The hosted platform once called LlamaCloud now goes by LlamaParse, according to LlamaIndex's own framework documentation ("LlamaParse (formerly LlamaCloud)"). That page points readers to developers.llamaindex.ai/llamaparse.
LlamaParse covers parsing, extraction, classification, splitting, and indexing.
A pull request to the LlamaIndex docs (run-llama/llama_index #23204, "LlamaCloud is now LlamaParse") says identifiers survived, including llama-cloud, LlamaCloud, LLAMA_CLOUD_API_KEY, while the packages llama-parse and llama-cloud-services are deprecated in favour of llama-cloud 2.x.
No exact rename date in results.
