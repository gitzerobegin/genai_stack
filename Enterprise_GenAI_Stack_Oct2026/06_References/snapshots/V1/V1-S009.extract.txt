Firecrawl announced a $75 million Series B on September 22, 2026, alongside the launch of Alexandria, its data platform for AI agents. Smash Capital led the round; Altos Ventures, Nexus Venture Partners, Y Combinator, Freestyle and Offline Ventures participated.
RuntimeWire: a Form D from Firecrawl's legal issuer, SideGuide Technologies Inc., showed $82,063,463 of preferred stock sold to seven investors, with the first sale recorded on August 31st. Firecrawl's announced Series B is about $7.1 million below the gross amount reported to the SEC, and neither disclosure explains the difference.
