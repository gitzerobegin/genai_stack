ClickHouse acquired Langfuse, and the deal was announced on January 16, 2026. ClickHouse's blog post describes Langfuse as the leading open-source platform for LLM observability, evaluations, and prompt management. The price was not disclosed.
Langfuse's own announcement (langfuse.com/blog/announcing-acquisition) says its roadmap is unchanged; it plans to keep Langfuse open source and self-hostable, with no planned changes to licensing.
Orrick (seller's law firm, January 2026) said Langfuse had more than 2,000 paying customers at the time of the deal.
