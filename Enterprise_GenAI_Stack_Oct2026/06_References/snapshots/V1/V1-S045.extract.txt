The Enterprise-Managed Authorization (EMA) extension (io.modelcontextprotocol/enterprise-managed-authorization) has reached stable status; blog announced it and the roadmap post describes it as stable. Clients obtain an ID-JAG from the enterprise IdP and exchange it for an access token. Adopted by Anthropic, Microsoft, Okta; VS Code support; Asana, Atlassian, Canva, Figma, Granola, Linear, Supabase support EMA. Extensions are opt-in.
