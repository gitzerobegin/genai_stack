The Linux Foundation formed the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) on December 9, 2025, with Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP), Block's goose and OpenAI's AGENTS.md as founding projects. Platinum members: AWS, Anthropic, Block, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI. Membership: 146 (March 2026), 190 (May 2026), 247 (August 2026). aaif.io blog "A2A joins AAIF's open agentic stack". Agentgateway also listed among projects. September 2026: Gates Foundation joined.
