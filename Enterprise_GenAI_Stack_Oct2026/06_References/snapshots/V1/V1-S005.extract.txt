Dynatrace completed its acquisition of Arize on October 1, 2026. The completion release was posted that afternoon at 4:05 p.m. EDT, according to Dynatrace's investor relations page.
Announcement: Dynatrace first revealed the deal on August 13, 2026, when it signed a definitive agreement to acquire Arize in a cash and stock transaction valued at $915 million.
Payment: About $815 million was to be paid in cash, with the rest in replacement equity awards for Arize employees joining Dynatrace.
Product plans: Arize's AX platform and the open-source Phoenix project continue to operate, and Arize capabilities will be folded into Dynatrace over time.
