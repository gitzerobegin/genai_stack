Skyflow, a data privacy vault company, raised $30 million as an extension of its Series B. Khosla Ventures led the round, with prior investors Mouro Capital, Foundation Capital and Canvas Ventures also participating. Skyflow's own news page dates the headline to April 5, 2024, while the initial announcement circulated in late March 2024 (Business Wire 28 March 2024).

Prior Series B was $45 million in 2021. Crunchbase data cited by Finovate puts total equity capital at $100 million. Customers named: GoodRx, Lenovo, Hippocratic AI. Uses "polymorphic encryption".
