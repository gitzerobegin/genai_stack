Llama Guard 4: a 12-billion-parameter multimodal safety classifier that handles both text and images, pruned from the Llama 4 Scout model; combines the capabilities of Llama Guard 3-8B and Llama Guard 3-11B-vision. Available through the Llama API's moderations endpoint; can screen prompts, responses, or both.
Llama Prompt Guard 2: two sizes, 86M (trained on English and non-English attacks) and 22M (smaller, faster, mostly English). Both target prompt injection and jailbreaks. "Llama Prompt Guard 2 22M reduces latency and compute costs by 75%, with minimal performance trade-offs." 512-token context window.
The Hugging Face model pages show the Llama Guard 4 and Prompt Guard 2 checkpoints last updated on April 29, 2025.
