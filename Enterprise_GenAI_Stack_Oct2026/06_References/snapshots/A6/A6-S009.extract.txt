The LiteLLM project released v1.83.0 on March 30, 2026, as a clean replacement for the two compromised PyPI packages.
The release notes describe it as the first LiteLLM release built and published through the new CI/CD v2 pipeline. Releases were paused for one week while a forensic review was completed with Mandiant and Veria Labs, the release pipeline was rebuilt with isolated environments and ephemeral credentials, and the codebase was checked for indicators of compromise.
Starting from v1.83.0-nightly, all LiteLLM Docker images published to GHCR are signed with cosign. Several vulnerability reports from independent researchers were all fixed in v1.83.0.
