Extract as returned by search tool (combined summary of Trend Micro, InfoQ, Cycode results):
The LiteLLM incident was a March 2026 compromise of the PyPI package, attributed to the TeamPCP threat group. Attackers pushed malicious versions 1.82.7 and 1.82.8 straight to PyPI, skipping the project's usual GitHub release path.
Trend Micro reports that the campaign spanned PyPI, npm, Docker Hub, GitHub Actions, and OpenVSX in a single coordinated operation. It harvested secrets: Trend Micro lists the targets as system information, environment variables, SSH keys, cloud provider credentials, and more.
InfoQ reports that the compromised version drew over 40 thousand downloads, and that PyPI quarantined the package about 40 minutes after it was reported. Download volume: InfoQ cites about 3 million downloads per day, while Trend Micro cites 3.4 million per day.
