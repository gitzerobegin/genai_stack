"The IORails engine is an optimized execution engine for supported input, output, and tool rails." Supported built-in flows: content safety check input / output, topic safety check input, jailbreak detection model. Configurations with custom flows or dialog rails fall back to the LLMRails engine. The engine is opt-in, enabled with NEMO_GUARDRAILS_IORAILS_ENGINE=1. Jailbreak detection (NIM) works on both engines.
