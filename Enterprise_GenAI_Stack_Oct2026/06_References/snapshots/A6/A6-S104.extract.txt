AWS announced on April 6, 2026 that the service now accepts human-readable aliases for policy stores; named policies and policy templates can be referenced by meaningful names. Available in every region where the service runs.
Cedar 4 is now required: "starting April 2026, the authorization APIs (IsAuthorized, BatchIsAuthorized, and their token-based variants) are being disabled for policy stores still on Cedar 2." Cedar 4 is not fully backward compatible (syntax changes, stricter validator, changed built-ins such as isInRange).
As of May 2026, Verified Permissions has aligned with Cedar and now supports multiple namespaces.
Cedar 4.5 support from August 2025. An AWS Security blog: "Enforce least-privilege authorization in multi-agent AI chains using Cedar".
