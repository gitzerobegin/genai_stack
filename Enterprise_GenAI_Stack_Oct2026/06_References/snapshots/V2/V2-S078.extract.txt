Article 50(2) marking duty for providers of generative systems already placed on the market before 2 August 2026: effective deadline 2 December 2026 (transitional provision in new Article 111(4)). Systems placed on the market on or after 2 August 2026 get no extension. Chatbot disclosure, deepfake disclosure and emotion-recognition notices required since 2 August 2026. Two new prohibitions under Article 5(1)(ba) and (bb) (nudification apps; CSAM generators) apply from 2 December 2026.
