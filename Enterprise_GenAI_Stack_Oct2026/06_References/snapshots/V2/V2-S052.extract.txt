The Critical Third Parties (Designation) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/777) made 8 July 2026; HM Treasury press release 10 July 2026. Designated from 13 July 2026: Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, Google Cloud EMEA Limited, Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited and Oracle Corporation UK Limited. Joint oversight by Bank of England, PRA and FCA. First self-assessments reportedly due within three months of designation (~mid-October 2026; single commentary). Further designations possible. One commentary says no power to fine the providers (unverified).
