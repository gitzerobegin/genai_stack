The EBA issued its final Guidelines EBA/GL/2026/09 on 18 September 2026. They will repeal the 2019 outsourcing guidelines (EBA/GL/2019/02) once they apply; until then the 2019 guidelines remain in force. Final but awaiting translation into EU official languages. Scope: non-ICT services from third-party providers (ICT under DORA Chapter V); "third-party arrangement" replaces "outsourcing" as organising concept; widened to payment and e-money institutions. Enhanced obligations for arrangements supporting critical or important functions. Register required. Date of application not yet fixed (Arthur Cox: effective date to be confirmed); critical/important arrangements to be reviewed within two years of the date of application (commentary).
