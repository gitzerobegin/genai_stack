========================================================================

The court gave final approval to the $1.5 billion settlement on July 20, 2026. Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín of the Northern District of California approved the settlement and entered final judgment, calling it "fair, reasonable, and adequate". She overruled all 53 objections. Fee award $101,561,111 (about 6.8% of the fund) instead of the 12.5% requested. Class members release only claims relating to past acquisition and copying through August 25, 2025; claims based on AI outputs are not released. Anthropic must destroy files torrented from LibGen or PiLiMi.
