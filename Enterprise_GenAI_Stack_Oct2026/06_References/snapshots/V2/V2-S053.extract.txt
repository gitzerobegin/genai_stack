PRA published PS7/26 in March 2026 with an updated SS2/21: "This SS is effective from 18 March 2027 and was published as part of PS7/26." New rules effective from 18 March 2027 require firms to notify regulators when entering into or significantly changing a Material Third-Party (MTP) arrangement and submit an annual register using standardised templates. Outsourcing/third-party section relevant to all PRA-regulated firms.
FCA published PS26/2 (Operational Incident and Third Party Reporting) on 18 March 2026, with FG26/3 and FG26/4. Final rules define a material third party arrangement, require notification of new or significantly changed arrangements and an annual register; no prescribed notification timeline but early notice expected. Dual-regulated firms make a single submission shared with both regulators (FCA Connect; RegData). Third-country branches excluded from notification but must submit register.
