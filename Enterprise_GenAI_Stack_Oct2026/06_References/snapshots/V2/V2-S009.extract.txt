Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon, a frontier model that is rolling out first to trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program. A broader public release is planned for later, after more testing.
The eventual general release is planned to start with paid API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers.
Fairwind is a limited access program for governments and trusted partners to use our cyber defense tools. Google reports more than 650 participating partners globally.
For trusted defenders and Google's internal teams, Google says it will release Argon without cyber guardrails.
The sources describe the 1-million-token limit inconsistently. Google's own announcement calls it a token limit, while a September recap ("The latest AI news we announced in September 2026") calls it an output limit.
Benchmarks are Google and Wiz internal.
