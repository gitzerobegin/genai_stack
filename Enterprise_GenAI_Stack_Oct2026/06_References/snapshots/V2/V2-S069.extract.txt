House passed S. 1071 with amendment December 10, 2025; Senate agreed December 17; signed December 18, 2025 as Public Law 119-60. Section 1532 "Guidance and Prohibition on Use of Certain Artificial Intelligence" entered into force subject to a 30-day grace period; tracker lists January 17, 2026 as date the prohibition took effect for DoD and its contractors. Covered AI: developed by DeepSeek or High Flyer and associated entities; also entities on specific lists or subject to foreign influence. Contractors restricted from using covered AI in performing DoD contracts (commercial guide), with case-by-case waivers. Section 6604: remove DeepSeek from intelligence community systems. Statute text not read.
