Astra debuted on September 3, 2026. OpenAI's release notes said access was rolling out to a limited set of organizations and Astra was not yet generally available. Broader availability was planned over the coming days.
OpenAI said Astra would reach all ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as through the OpenAI API, Microsoft Azure, and AWS Bedrock. A staff reply in the OpenAI developer forum later said Astra is now rolled out to all Plus and Business users too.

GPT-6 Pro, powered by GPT-6 Astra, is available in ChatGPT for Pro $100, Pro $200, Business and Enterprise plans. Enterprise administrators can enable Astra for their workspace; access is off by default at launch.
Access to Astra for advanced cybersecurity workflows will initially be available to a small group of alpha testers, with access through Daybreak Blue expanding afterward.
