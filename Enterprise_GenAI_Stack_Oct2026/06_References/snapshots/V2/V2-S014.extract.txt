Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max as a hosted model on August 3, 2026 (MarkTechPost 3 Aug 2026; Quartz), and released its open weights on August 12 as Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B under a custom license, not Apache 2.0. 2.4 trillion parameter MoE, 95B active, 1M context, native text, image and video input. Previewed at WAIC Shanghai in July as Qwen3.8-Max-Preview.
Open weights version reportedly limited to text input/output and not the full 1M context.
Qwen3.8-27B: Hugging Face Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B under Apache 2.0, 262k native context (The Decoder: "Alibaba's Qwen team releases Qwen 3.8 models with open weights under the Apache 2.0 license").
