Search-tool summary of developers.openai.com/codex/config-advanced and /codex/config-reference:
- Custom providers are defined under [model_providers.<id>] and selected with model_provider. The docs' example includes a local Ollama entry (base_url http://localhost:11434/v1) and an Azure entry using the "responses" wire API.
- Built-in provider IDs openai, ollama and lmstudio are reserved and cannot be overridden.
- Project-level .codex/config.toml files ignore model_provider and model_providers; put provider, notification and telemetry keys in user-level config.
- The --oss flag is the shortcut for local models; oss_provider picks the default local provider (lmstudio | ollama). Ollama's docs show `codex --oss -m gpt-oss:120b`.
