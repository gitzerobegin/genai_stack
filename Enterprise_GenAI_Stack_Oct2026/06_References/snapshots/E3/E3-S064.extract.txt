- SP 800-218A (final, July 2024) is a Community Profile for generative AI and dual-use foundation models that augments the practices and tasks in SSDF version 1.1 with recommendations, considerations, notes and informative references for generative AI development.
- PW.5.1 (secure coding) is expanded to include AI technology-specific considerations, e.g. carefully coding the handling of inputs (including prompts and user data) and outputs.
- Its scope is developing AI models and systems; it is not guidance on developers using AI coding assistants.
