- The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) launched on 9 December 2025 with three founding projects: Anthropic's Model Context Protocol, Block's goose and OpenAI's AGENTS.md; AAIF is a directed fund under the Linux Foundation.
- OpenAI co-founded the foundation with Anthropic and Block and donated AGENTS.md, a Markdown-based format giving coding agents project-specific instructions; OpenAI released AGENTS.md in August 2025 and said more than 60,000 open-source projects had adopted it by the launch (project-reported).
- Later 2026 Linux Foundation materials list A2A, agentgateway and Agent Router among AAIF projects.
