Search-tool summary of cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/careful-adoption-agentic-si-services (URL slug as published), cyber.gov.au publication page and PDF (media.defense.gov/2026/Apr/30/2003922823/-1/-1/0/CAREFUL ADOPTION OF AGENTIC AI SERVICES_FINAL.PDF), and NCSC UK blog "Thinking carefully before adopting agentic AI":
- Joint publication dated 1 May 2026, co-authored by Australia's ASD's ACSC, CISA, NSA, the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, New Zealand's NCSC and the UK's NCSC.
- Covers cybersecurity challenges and risks of introducing agentic AI into IT environments and best practices for securing those systems; mainly targets LLM-based agentic systems.
- Recommends deploying incrementally, limiting agents to low-risk tasks and avoiding broad or unrestricted access to sensitive data or critical systems; strict privilege controls, continuous monitoring, strong identity management, human oversight and alignment with existing cyber-security frameworks.
- Recommends building security in from design and using agentic AI risk taxonomies such as the OWASP GenAI Security Project and MITRE ATLAS.
- Secondary (Mayer Brown, 10 June 2026): about 30 pages with more than 100 recommendations organised by lifecycle stage; described as the first Five Eyes cyber-security guidance specifically on agentic AI. No coding-assistant-specific content identified.
